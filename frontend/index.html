    How this score is built

    Skillmark is a rule-based NLP pipeline written in plain JavaScript. It splits the text into lines and tokens, reduces words to stems, finds section headings, matches skills and their aliases against a role profile, and measures keyword overlap with a job description. The 1 to 10 rating is the weighted total divided by ten. It estimates what an ATS and a recruiter skim for. It is not any vendor's real ATS, so treat it as a checklist.
